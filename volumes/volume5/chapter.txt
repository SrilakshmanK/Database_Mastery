📘 Volume 05 — NoSQL, Caching & the Modern Data Stack
🎯 Objective

Move beyond pure relational thinking and become comfortable choosing, designing, and operating the right database for the right job — document stores, caches, and hybrid architectures — the way real production systems are actually built.

Outcome:

After completing this volume, you should be able to:
Explain when NOT to use PostgreSQL.
Design MongoDB schemas for real applications.
Use Redis correctly as a cache, not just a key-value toy.
Integrate an ORM properly instead of fighting it.
Design a multi-tenant SaaS data layer.
Defend a polyglot persistence decision in an interview.

🎯 Volume Challenge

Without watching a tutorial:

Take one existing PostgreSQL project (yours or the LMS) and redesign part of it as a polyglot system.

Requirements:
Identify at least 2 pieces of data better suited to MongoDB than PostgreSQL.
Move them and justify the decision in writing.
Add a Redis caching layer for at least 3 hot read paths.
Measure and document the performance difference (before/after).
Handle cache invalidation correctly on writes.
