📘 Volume 06 — AI-Native Databases & Database DevOps
🎯 Objective

Become the engineer who understands both sides of the AI-era divide: the data infrastructure that powers AI features (vector search, RAG), and the operational discipline (DevOps for databases) that AI cannot replace. This is the volume that makes your database skills future-proof rather than commoditized.

Outcome:

After completing this volume, you should be able to:
Explain what a vector embedding is and why similarity search needs a different index.
Build a working RAG pipeline using pgvector.
Critically review AI-generated SQL instead of blindly trusting it.
Treat database migrations as code, tested and versioned like application code.
Provision a database with Infrastructure as Code.
Discuss data governance and compliance like someone accountable for it, not just someone who writes queries.
