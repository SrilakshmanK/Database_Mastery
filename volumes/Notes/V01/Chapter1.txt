What is data, and what is the difference between data and information?

Data is a representation of facts, measurements, observations, events, or values. Data by itself may not provide meaningful context. When we add
context and meaning to data, it becomes information.

Can you give me a real-world example of data and information?

172 is just a number. It could represent height, weight, or a count, but without context, we don't know what it means. When we add the context 
"Height: 172", it becomes understandable information.  

What is the difference between a database and a DBMS?

A database is an organized collection of data that can be stored, retrieved, and modified. A DBMS is the software that manages the database and allows 
users or applications to interact with it. It acts as an intermediary between the application and the database.
