🏆 Capstone Milestone (LMS Project)

Before writing any SQL:

Design the complete LMS database.
Identify all entities (Users, Courses, Lessons, Enrollments, Payments, etc.).
Draw the ER diagram.
Normalize to 3NF.
Document your design decisions.
List the indexes you plan to create later in PostgreSQL.

Store everything in a /database-design folder inside your LMS repository.

💼 Career Layer

Push your database designs to GitHub with meaningful commits.

Examples:

docs: add ER diagram for LMS database
design: normalize course and enrollment tables to 3NF
docs: document primary and foreign key decisions
docs: explain indexing strategy for user and course tables

This demonstrates to recruiters that you don't just know SQL—you understand database design and engineering.
